The app is an offline study tool for the Vietnamese driving licence theory test. No account or login is needed. The practice videos tab and the driving schools tab need an internet connection. Pro is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase (pro_lifetime); tap the info icon at the top of the home screen > "Bản Pro", or open any Pro feature, to see the purchase screen, which also has a "Khôi phục giao dịch" (Restore Purchases) button. This app is not affiliated with any government agency; this is stated in the description and on the About screen.
